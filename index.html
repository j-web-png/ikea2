Analysis, Patterns & Limitations

Operating Leverage Squeeze: While top-line revenue contracted by only -1.68% (€0.44B), operating profit dropped by -15.42% (€0.27B). This amplified impact occurs because Inter IKEA held wholesale prices stable or reduced them to support consumer affordability, while structural operating expenses (wage inflation, energy, digital investments, and restructuring) expanded.

Net Profit Cushioning: Net profit fell by only -6.48% (€1.40B vs. €1.50B) due to higher net financial income supported by currency derivative gains, cushioning the operating compression.

Project Limitations: Restricted to $N=2$ complete annual reports, limiting visibility into intra-year seasonal volatility. Furthermore, Inter IKEA Group's wholesale figures are decoupled from Ingka Group's retail store margins.

Strategic Recommendations

  • 1. Cap Non-Core OpEx: Set a strict OpEx ceiling tied to digital automation efficiencies post-restructuring.
  • 2. Vertical Integration: Utilize recently acquired sustainable forestland in the Baltics to insulate timber sourcing costs.
  • 3. Digital & Franchise Growth: Accelerate urban plan-and-order points to lift franchise sales volume and zero-COGS fee revenue.
Project Scope: Feasible student project built for static deployment on GitHub Pages or Vercel.
